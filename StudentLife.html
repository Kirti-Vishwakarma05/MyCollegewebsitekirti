Student Life at Pragnya Degree & PG College

Campus life at Pragnya College is designed to foster both academic success and personal growth. Students participate in diverse extra-curricular activities, technical workshops, and community events.

Academic Flexibility

  • Diverse course streams across Computer Science, Commerce, and Management.
  • Choice-based elective subjects tailored to industry needs.

Skill Development

  • Seminars by industry experts and guest lecturers.
  • Certification programs in digital productivity and communication skills.

Festivals and Events

  • Annual cultural fests highlighting music, dance, and drama.
  • Sports meets and indoor game tournaments.

Career Guidance & Placement

  • Dedicated placement cell offering interview preparation.
  • Resume-building sessions and mock technical interviews.
  • On-campus recruitment drives by top IT and corporate firms.
