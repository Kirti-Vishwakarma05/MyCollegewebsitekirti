Departments

Computer Science

The Department of Computer Science offers specialized programs in: BCA, B.Sc Data Science, and M.Sc Computer Science. Students gain practical experience in programming and database management.

Mathematics

The Department of Mathematics provides foundational and advanced coursework for B.Sc streams including MSCs and MCCs. The curriculum emphasizes analytical reasoning, Algebra, Linear Algebra, Calculus, and Differential equations.

Statistics

Department of Statistics offers core courses integrated into B.Sc degree programs. The curriculum focuses on data analysis, probability theory, statistical inference, sample surveys, operations research, statistical modeling and practical applications.

Commerce

Department of Commerce offers B.Com in Computer Applications, Taxation,as well as M.Com. The curriculum focuses on accounting, finance, adn corporate management.
