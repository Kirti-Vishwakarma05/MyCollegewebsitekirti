Pragnya Degree & PG College Details

Established in 2011, Pragnya Degree & PG College is affiliated with Osmania University and recognized for its high academic standards and student-centric approach. The college offers undergraduate and postgraduate degree programs including B.Com (Computer Applications,Taxation), BBA, BCA, B.Sc (Data Science, MSCs, MCCs), M.Sc, and M.Com. Equipped with modern computer laboratories, biometric attendance systems, and campus-wide CCTV surveillance, the institution provides an ideal environment for professional development.
