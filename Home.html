About Our College

Pragnya Degree & PG College is an educational institution located in Chandanagar, Hyderabad.
We are committed to delivering high-quality education across undergraduate and postgraduate programs.
Our campus offers modern facilities, dedicated faculty, and a supportive environment.

History of Pragnya Degree College

Pragnya Degree & PG College has grown into a highly respected academic center in Hyderabad.
Over the years, the institution has expanded its curriculum.
It continues to empower students through career-driven skill development and strong industry connections.
